# The door catalogue

Every door an outside holder can present a signed act at — forty-six, in twenty-one
files — in one table: the
`Act` kind (`src/sim/systems/present.ts`), the door it reaches, the bytes
the door verifies and the order of that body function's arguments, the
door's refusals in the order the door's own comment lists them (quoted, not
rewritten), and the entries of `public/doors/vectors.json` — or, for the
three War Bay hire doors, the three task doors, the two appeal doors, the four sanction doors, the citation door, the chamber's two doors, the two co-founding doors, the six guild doors, the relief door and the two credit doors, of `public/doors/vectors-small.json` — that cover it.

`present(world, act)` is the one caller. It lives in `sim/systems/` because
ten of the doors — admission, leaving, adoption, the governor's policy,
the two project doors, the work and the three War Bay hire doors — are the sim's and
take a `World`, and the rest are the agent layer's: the seam that knows both
sides, as the economy's laws do, so the agent layer stays free of the sim.
It passes `world.tick` into every tick slot and lets each door read
`realmOf(world)` itself: no act carries a tick or a realm as an argument.
The two mandate drafts (`signedMandate.draft`, `admit.opening.draft`) carry
`issuedAt`/`expiresAt` inside the signed body, and the passport carries
`city`; their doors check those against the world's tick and name
(`mandates.ts`: `issuedAt !== host.tick`; `admission.ts`: the opening's
`issuedAt`, `passport.city`). It verifies nothing and writes nothing:
`{ ok: true, rows }` is the seqs the chain gained, read off `audit.count`
before and after the door; `{ ok: false, reason }` is the door's sentence
untouched — its one sentence of its own is `no such door: <kind>` for a
kind off the wire that names none. `bodyOf(world, act)` returns the bytes
to sign for that act at the world's tick and realm, ignoring any signature
already on the act; where a body carries a sequence it reads it through
the door's own reader (`leavingSeqOf`, `claimSeqOf`, `actsOf`,
`adoptionSeqOf`, `policySeqOf`).

The vectors are written by `npx tsx bench/doors-vectors.ts` on seed 42,
population 2000, `mind: declineFounding`, the holder `vectors:42` named
governor at the founding (`WorldOptions.governor`, 2026-09-14 — `start.governor`
in the file, and the replayer founds the world with it), from tick 3420
(day 3, 09:00),
and replayed top to bottom by `tests/present.test.ts`; entry numbers below
are positions in `entries`. Every refusal vector is a signature made for
another realm (`nowhere:Elsewhere:0`), except the countersignature, whose
body carries no realm and is refused under another holder's key. Five
holders sign them, keyed from public seeds: `vectors:42` (who does
everything, and holds the governor's seat), `:respondent`, `:arbiter`, `:adopter`, who is never
admitted and adopts Yusuf Rossi — resident 0 of seed 42 at 2,000, the first
adult with a job and no firm, the seat `tests/adoption.test.ts` takes — and
`:applicant`, who is never admitted either and applies at the registration
door (2026-09-15), where the governor approves it; the holder then opens a
shared project at the grocer's building and the respondent funds it
(2026-09-16).

The second file, `public/doors/vectors-small.json`, is written by
`npx tsx bench/doors-vectors-small.ts` on the small layout (D4, 2026-09-17):
`seed 42, size 96, population 100, layout: 'small'`, `mind: declineFounding`,
the holder `vectors-small:42` named governor, from tick 0 — its own start
hash, because the 2,000-person world lays no Boz Institute and no seat there
can earn the credential a hire requires (`start.size` and `start.layout` are
in the mark, and the replayer founds the world with them). It follows the
fixture of `tests/hire.test.ts`: four holders admitted at tick 0 (`:hirer`
— the seed `vectors-small:42` — `:hired`, `:evaluator`, `:other`) miss the
seek, enrol and graduate together at tick 10,980 (a `ticks` step), the hirer
founds a firm in the closed grocer's premise (a `foundFirm` step: the
fixture's closing, a stake from the treasury, the founding mandate signed
under the holder's public seed — not a door), and then the three hire doors
in phase order, each a wrong-realm refusal then a landing, with a `ticks`
step to the first Friday 17:00 payday between the acceptance and the
evaluation so delivery lands by law; then `:applicant` applies at the
registration door and the governor approves it. Entry numbers for those
doors below are positions in that file. Because
`present` has no tick slot, a body signed for another tick or with a stale
sequence reaches the same refusal — "does not verify" — and never the
door's own "signed for tick X; the world is at Y"; `tests/doors.test.ts`
("one caller into every seam") asserts that for the halt, and asserts the
draft's own tick at its door: a `signedMandate` issued at `tick + 1` is
refused by the register with nothing moved.
